How Saturday Shift Works

The 5-Week Rotation Cycle

Saturday Shift uses a modern round-robin pairing algorithm designed to rotate shifts perfectly. With 5 employees, the sequence of assignments matches the user-provided rotation precisely:

  • Week 1: Kuku & Dagi
  • Week 2: Nesti & Tame
  • Week 3: Mel & Kuku
  • Week 4: Dagi & Nesti
  • Week 5: Tame & Mel
  • ... then repeats starting with Week 6 (Kuku & Dagi).

Dynamic Features

Unlike a static chart, Saturday Shift is highly interactive:

  • Employee Ordering: Change the sequence of employee names. The rotation immediately recalculates with the new sequence.
  • Vacation Mode: Exclude an employee for specific dates. The app skips them and assigns the next person in line, making sure everyone still gets a fair number of turns over the year.
  • Overrides: Lock specific people into specific Saturdays without breaking the overall pattern for other weeks.
  • Shift Exchanges: Swap shifts between two Saturdays (e.g. if Kuku swaps with Nesti, Nesti works this week and Kuku works next week).
  • Export: Download the complete schedule in CSV format for Excel, Google Sheets, or calendars.
